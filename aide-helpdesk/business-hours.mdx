Business hours tell Aide when your team is around. They're used by SLAs, routing rules, handoff messages, notifications and Aide Chat's outside-hours behaviour. Set them in Settings > Helpdesk > Business hours.

## Schedules

A schedule has a name, a time zone and, for each weekday, opening and closing times or Closed. One schedule is the default: teams, SLAs and rules without their own schedule use it. You can create more schedules, for example for a team in another country, and pick them where a schedule is asked for (an SLA policy, or Aide Chat > Behaviour). Each schedule shows what uses it.

## Holidays

Add the days you're closed, up to 200 per schedule. To save typing, import the public holidays for a year: enter a country code (US, GB, CA, FR, DE, AU, IE, NL or ES) and click **Import**, then remove the days you work on.

## What customers are told

Each schedule has a **Reply-time message inside hours** ("We usually reply within an hour.") and a **Reply-time message outside hours**. Handoff messages use your hours through the `{{reply_time}}` variable: outside hours it becomes your next opening time, so a customer writing at night knows when to expect an answer.
