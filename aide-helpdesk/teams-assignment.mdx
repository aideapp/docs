Teams are groups of teammates that conversations route to, such as "Returns" or "Wholesale". Assignment decides who inside a team owns each conversation.

## Create a team

In Settings > Helpdesk > Teams, create a team, give it a name and an emoji, and add teammates (invite them under Settings > Teammates first). For each team you can also set:

- **Business hours**: the workspace default or another schedule.
- **Notify**: everyone on the team when a conversation arrives, or only the assignee, once assigned.
- **Team signature**: used as `{{ team.signature }}` in reply templates.

## How conversations are assigned

Pick an assignment method per team, or leave it on the workspace default set in Settings > Helpdesk > Assignment:

- **Manual**: conversations wait in the team view until someone picks them up.
- **Round robin**: each conversation that needs a person goes to the next available member in turn.
- **Balanced**: goes to whoever has the fewest open conversations, up to their capacity. Set a **Capacity per member** so nobody is overloaded.

Conversations the AI Agent is answering are assigned to Aide; they reach a team when Aide hands off or a routing rule sends them there.

## Workspace assignment defaults

In Settings > Helpdesk > Assignment:

- **Self-assign on reply** (on by default): replying to an unassigned conversation assigns it to you.
- **Unassign when returning to Aide**: Return to Aide clears the assignee.
- **Reassign when a member goes away**: open conversations on a member who sets themselves Away move back to their team's queue.
- **Automatic away after**: minutes of inactivity in the dashboard before a member is marked Away, off by default.
- **Default method for teams without one**.

## Your availability

Set yourself as away from the sidebar, with a reason: busy, in a meeting, on a break, done for the day, out of office or sick. Round robin and balanced assignment skip away members.
