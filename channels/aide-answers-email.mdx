Your AI agent can answer the support email you bring into Aide, not just draft replies for your team. It uses the same knowledge, order tools and handoff rules as chat.

## Turn it on

1. Connect your support address in Channels > Email, by forwarding or with Gmail. A new address starts with your agent switched on in "Test senders only", with your own email as the test sender.
2. Once the agent is deployed, Aide replies to your own emails to the address and writes a private draft for every customer email, sending customers nothing.
3. When the drafts look right, set "How Aide answers" to **Everyone**: Aide then answers new customer emails nobody on your team has taken.

One agent can answer chat, email and your helpdesk at once. Each address has one agent: in Aide AI Agent, "Where it answers" switches an address on or off, and switching on an address another agent answers moves it to this one. A new address gets your deployed agent, or the workspace's starting agent before any agent is deployed. Without an agent, emails go to your team with a Copilot draft.

## Try it first

In Channels > Email, "How Aide answers" sets who gets Aide's replies on each address:

- **Shadow: draft only, never send:** Aide writes each reply as a private note and sends nothing, so your team can compare.
- **Test senders only:** only the addresses you list (or `@domain` for everyone at a domain) get replies. A new address starts here, with the email of the teammate who added it.
- **A share of conversations:** a percentage of conversations get replies.
- **Everyone**: every customer gets replies. Addresses added before October 2026 use this unless you changed it.

Outside the rollout, emails get a private draft instead.

"Quiet period (seconds)" makes Aide wait after a customer's email (up to 600 seconds; by default it doesn't wait), so two emails in a row get one reply that answers both.

## What the reply looks like

It opens with the customer's first name when Aide knows it, answers in full paragraphs, and ends with the agent's signature, set in Aide AI Agent > Email (your team name when it's empty). Products, orders and tracking appear as text and links.

## Orders by email

Aide acts only on orders placed with the address the email came from. Order actions it offers ask the customer to reply to confirm.

## When Aide steps back

- When Aide hands off, the customer gets one email with your handoff message and the conversation goes to your team.
- Once a teammate is assigned or takes over, Aide stops replying on that thread.
- Aide never answers bounces, auto-replies, mailing-list and bulk mail, no-reply senders, or mail from your own support address.
- If a thread gets three AI replies within an hour, Aide hands it to your team with an internal note.

Each email Aide sends counts as one AI reply, shown as "Aide Email" in usage.
