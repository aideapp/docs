Aide's replies on WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram use each app's own interactive messages instead of plain text with links. When a customer taps a button, Aide acts on it straight away, so they don't have to type. There's nothing to set up.

## What customers get

- **Products:** several products go out as a swipeable carousel (up to 10); one product as a card with a button to its page.
- **Orders and tracking:** a card with "Track package".
- **Returns:** a card with "Start your return" when you use a returns portal.
- **Suggested replies:** buttons or a list to pick from.
- **"Talk to a person":** tapping it hands the conversation to your team at once.

Button labels are in the customer's language and shortened to fit each app's limits.

## Confirming an order change with a tap

When Aide offers an order action, such as cancelling an order, the customer gets yes and no buttons. A yes runs it, as long as the shopper is still verified for that order. On a topic set to Draft for review, a yes sends it to a teammate instead. Either way, Aide tells the customer what happened.

## Ratings

"Did that solve it?" and satisfaction ratings are asked with buttons, and the tap is recorded without another reply. On WhatsApp the rating is a five-option list; on Messenger it's Meta's own feedback form, which needs your store's privacy policy link; on Instagram it's quick replies.

## Sharing an email or phone number

A Messenger customer Aide can't yet match to your store gets buttons to share their email or phone number (phone only on Instagram), with a line saying they're for finding their orders. Tapping one fills it in from their Meta account and counts as verified. A typed address goes through the usual check.

## Good to know

- When Aide is about to answer, the customer sees their message marked seen and Aide typing. This never happens in shadow or for your team's replies.
- If an app refuses a rich message, Aide sends the same content once in a simpler form, so the customer always gets the answer.
- A tap on an expired offer, or on a button from another conversation, is read as ordinary text.
- In the conversation, your team sees what the customer actually got under each Aide reply, with the option they tapped ticked.
