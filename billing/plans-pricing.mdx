## What each plan adds

Every plan includes every feature: Aide Chat on your storefront, the AI Agent answering from products and policies, Shopify order lookups and order actions (refunds, cancellations, address changes), Copilot drafts, and Aide's drafts and replies in Zendesk, Gorgias or Front. Plans differ in how many AI replies they include, and in:

- **Free**: Aide Helpdesk for up to 2 team members, 1 store. Shows "Powered by Aide".
- **Starter**: unlimited team members, your own branding on Aide Chat, 1 store.
- **Pro**: up to 3 stores, a setup call with us, security questionnaire support, and a 99.9% availability target.
- **Scale**: unlimited stores, a dedicated account lead, monthly performance reviews (bi-weekly through peak season), a 99.9% SLA with service credits, security review support and a BAA.
- **Enterprise**: sold by our team, with limits agreed per contract.

Every plan learns your full catalog, with no product limit.

## Pay yearly

Starter, Pro and Scale can be paid yearly instead of monthly, for about 20% less:

| Plan    | Price per year | Works out at | Saves a year |
| ------- | -------------- | ------------ | ------------ |
| Starter | $372           | $31 a month  | $96          |
| Pro     | $1,428         | $119 a month | $360         |
| Scale   | $4,788         | $399 a month | $1,200       |

Choose **Annual** above the plans when you pick one, or **Switch to annual** in **Settings > Billing**.

- Your AI replies still reset every month, with the same monthly allowance and the same monthly cap on extra replies. An annual plan doesn't pool a year of replies.
- Extra replies are billed monthly, on their own invoice, only in months you go over.
- The 7-day trial works the same way. When it ends, your card is charged for the year.

## Extra replies and the monthly cap

On paid plans, if you go over the AI replies your plan includes in a month, each extra reply is charged at your plan's rate, so you aren't cut off during peak season. Extra charges stop at a monthly cap: $110 on Starter, $350 on Pro and $1,000 on Scale. When the cap is reached, AI replies pause until the cycle resets. On the Free plan, 150 AI replies a month is a hard limit: AI replies stop when you reach it.

Copilot drafts count as AI replies too, and so do drafts Aide keeps inside Aide while a helpdesk inbox is in shadow (or on a conversation a partial rollout leaves in shadow). While AI replies are paused, new conversations go to your team, customers see the "While AI replies are paused" message from your handoff settings, and Aide stops all other AI work on the workspace too (Copilot, tagging, titles, field collection and previews) until the cycle resets or the plan changes. **What counts as a billable AI reply** lists what stops.

## Free trial

Starter, Pro and Scale start with a 7-day trial with a set number of AI replies: 250 on Starter, 1,000 on Pro and 3,750 on Scale. If you need the full allowance sooner, choose **Start plan now** in **Settings > Billing** to end the trial and start paying.

## Change or cancel your plan

Owners and admins manage billing in **Settings > Billing**:

- **Upgrade** or **Change plan** at any time.
- **Manage payment and invoices** opens the billing portal.
- **Cancel plan** keeps your plan until the end of the current billing period. Until then, **Keep my plan** undoes the cancellation.

On a yearly plan:

- **Upgrading**, or switching from monthly to yearly, applies right away. You're charged today for the rest of the year, less what's left of the period you already paid for. A switch from monthly keeps your billing date, so the year starts on it.
- **Moving to a lower plan**, or from yearly back to monthly, applies at the end of your year. Until then you keep everything you paid for.
- **Cancelling** keeps your plan until the end of the year, then the workspace moves to Free. Yearly plans aren't refunded for the months left.
- We email you before your year renews.

## Paying through Shopify

A workspace with a Shopify store connected and no paid card plan is billed through Shopify: the plan, trial and extra AI replies appear on the store's Shopify bill. You choose, change or cancel the plan on Shopify's plan page, opened from Aide's billing settings or from Aide in your Shopify admin. Workspaces already paying by card keep that until their plan ends. See [Pay for Aide through Shopify](/billing/shopify-billing).

- Only replies over the allowance are charged to Shopify, at your plan's rate and up to its cap.
- Uninstalling the Aide app from Shopify ends the subscription, and the workspace falls back to Free.
- If Shopify freezes the subscription over a billing issue, AI replies pause after 7 days, as with a failed card payment.
